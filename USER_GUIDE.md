# Ethiopia Arenguade Paper Product — User Operations Guide

Welcome to the Ethiopia Arenguade Paper Product platform operations guide. This manual provides simple, step-by-step instructions for the 5 most common tasks for each role (**Customer**, **Teacher / Artisan**, and **Admin**).

---

## Part 1: Customer Guide

### 1. Order Custom Paper Bags or Packaging
1. Open the **Shop** tab from the bottom navigation bar (or top menu).
2. Tap on your desired bag type (e.g. *Kraft Carry Bags* or *Takeout Sacks*).
3. Choose your quantity and enter your delivery address in Addis Ababa.
4. Transfer payment via **CBE** or **Telebirr**, upload your receipt screenshot, and tap **"Confirm Order"**.

### 2. Track Your Order Status
1. Tap the **Orders** tab in the bottom bar.
2. Select your active order to open the status tracker.
3. Review the current stage: *Payment Verification* → *In Production* → *Ready for Delivery* → *Delivered*.

### 3. Register for a Paper Crafting Masterclass
1. Tap the **Classes** tab in the bottom navigation.
2. Browse upcoming in-person workshops or interactive live masterclasses.
3. Tap **"Register for Class"**, transfer the registration fee, upload your receipt, and tap **"Submit Registration"**.

### 4. Join a Scheduled Live Stream Session
1. Tap **Classes** and select your confirmed live class session.
2. When the session is active, tap the prominent green **"Join Live Studio"** button.
3. Grant camera and microphone permissions when prompted, and tap **"Raise Hand"** if you have a question.

### 5. Submit Custom Artwork or Branding
1. Tap your **Account** tab and select **"Custom Design Submission"**.
2. Tap the upload area to take a photo of your sketch or select your brand logo file.
3. Provide your bag dimensions and notes, then tap **"Submit Design Request"**.

## Part 2: Teacher / Artisan Instructor Guide

### 1. View Today's Scheduled Workshops
1. Sign in to the Instructor Portal; your **Today** dashboard opens immediately.
2. View your assigned morning or afternoon sessions without needing to search.

### 2. Launch a Live Stream Workshop
1. On your **Today** screen, tap the green **"Open Studio"** button on the active session card.
2. Verify your camera and microphone preview in the audio-visual check dialog.
3. Tap **"Go Live"** to start broadcasting to enrolled students.

### 3. Manage Student Interaction During Live Class
1. Watch the right-hand panel for student hand-raises or chat questions.
2. Tap **"Invite to Speak"** next to a student's name to unmute them for audio feedback.
3. Tap **"Mute All"** at any time to resume uninterrupted demonstration.

### 4. Take Attendance for an In-Person Workshop
1. In your **Classes** list, tap **"Take Attendance"** on the workshop card.
2. Tap the green checkmark next to each attendee present in the studio.
3. Tap **"Save Attendance"** to finalize the roster records.

### 5. Review Class Enrollment Rosters
1. Tap **My Classes** in the navigation bar.
2. Select any past or upcoming session to inspect the confirmed student list and contact phone numbers.

## Part 3: Factory Administrator Guide

### 1. Process Pending Payments (Unified Queue)
1. From the Admin Home Action Inbox, tap **"Process Payments"** (or the **Payments** bottom tab).
2. Inspect the payment receipt proof image on the left/top.
3. Tap the primary green **"Approve"** button to clear the settlement, or tap **"Reject"** and pick a reason.
4. Tap **"Next"** to immediately review the next pending settlement in the queue.

### 2. Advance an Order to Production and Fulfillment
1. Tap **Orders** in the navigation bar and select the order requiring action.
2. Under order details, tap the single prominent **"Move to Next Status"** button (e.g. *"Start Production"* or *"Mark Ready"*).
3. The customer receives an immediate status update and SMS notification.

### 3. Create or Edit a Masterclass Workshop
1. Tap **Classes** in the bottom navigation and tap **"+ New Class"**.
2. Choose between **Live Online Stream** or **In-Person Workshop**.
3. Fill in the title, date/time, fee (ETB), capacity, and assigned instructor.
4. Tap **"Publish Class"** to make it available for customer registration.

### 4. Add or Update a Product in the Catalog
1. Tap **More** → **Product Catalog**.
2. Tap **"+ New Product"**, enter the name, description, unit price, and upload product photos.
3. Toggle **"Available"** or **"Featured"** directly in the product list to update website availability instantly.

### 5. Manage Staff Roles and Factory Settings
1. Tap **More** → **Users & Staff**.
2. Search for any user by name, email, or phone number.
3. Tap on the user, select their new role (*Admin*, *Teacher*, or *Customer*), and tap **"Confirm Role Change"**.
